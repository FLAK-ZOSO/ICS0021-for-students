## This repository

What you will find here is...

- `Homework.md` files, containing formal specifications for homeworks

Optionally, some weeks may provide...

- `Lecture.md` with a very raw list of the topics/commands covered
- `Practice.md` files and some scripts, containing the formal specifications for classwork and to make it easier for you to follow along
- `Questions.md` files, containing a listing of mainly theoretical questions to validate your knowledge after studying

Each one of these files will be sorted by week, for instance the homework specifications from week 4 will be available at `04/Homework.md` in this repository.

### Notes

Sometimes, due to aboundance or shortage of time or other factors, some material listed in a `Lecture.md` is actually covered in the practice, or vice versa, or even at an earlier/later week.

The `Lecture.md`/`Practice.md` files were used as a checklist by us and are thus "raw": you will find them barely discoursive and written in the first person. We are still providing (some of) them as they are, unless you prefer an AI-polished version.
